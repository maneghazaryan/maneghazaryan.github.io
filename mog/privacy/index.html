Privacy Policy

Mog · Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: our games and apps do not collect, store on servers, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. Your progress stays on your device.

This policy applies to all games and apps published by Mog on Google Play and the App Store (together, the "Games"), listed below. "We", "us" and "our" refer to the developer, Mog.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The Games do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and do not require you to create an account.

Data stored on your device

The Games save things like your best score, game progress and settings (for example, sound on/off) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in your device settings.

Advertising, analytics and purchases

The Games currently contain no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools and no in-app purchases.

If a future version of a Game adds advertising or any other third-party service that processes data, we will update this policy before that version is released. The update will name the service, describe what data it uses and why, and explain your choices (such as resetting your advertising ID). The Game's Data safety section on Google Play will be updated at the same time.

Permissions

The Games do not request access to sensitive device permissions such as location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage of your personal files.

Google Play

The Games are downloaded and updated through Google Play, which is operated by Google. Google may collect information when you use Google Play, as described in the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive personal information about you from Google.

Children's privacy

Because the Games do not collect personal information from anyone, they do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell, rent, trade or share any personal information, because we do not collect any.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove all locally saved game data at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing its data in your device settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when a new game is published or a new feature is added. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
